FR

Le but de HAZAK

Le but de HAZAK est de rendre le judaïsme accessible à tous. Située à Lausanne, l'organisation Hazak s'efforce de faire que les étudiants se sentent les bienvenus et fassent partie de la communauté. Avec des efforts de sensibilisation, les juifs d'âge et de type différents peuvent tous se retrouver en HAZAK.

EN

Mission Statement

Hazak, inspired by the Lubavitcher Rebbe and in the Chabad approach, is dedicated to providing a positive and enriching experience for every Jew seeking to connect with their heritage. Our mission is to empower individuals by sharing the knowledge and information that rightfully belongs to them, making the timeless wisdom of the Torah accessible and meaningful to all.

Le mot du Rabbin

A Case for Shmurah Matzah

from the desk of Rabbi M. Mendel Naparstek

Dear Friends,

As we approach this year's Passover, we are reminded that this holiday is not just a time for remembrance, but a time for deep, visceral experience. Through the seder, we immerse ourselves in the story of the Exodus, reliving the journey of our ancestors as if it were our own.

Read more

Our sages say that one who is meticulous with the customs of Pessa'h will be protected from sin for the rest of the year. It is for this reason that many prefer to use shmura matzah; the "rolex" of all matzah. The term "shmura" means "watched" in Hebrew, referring to the careful supervision of the entire process, from harvesting the wheat to baking the matza, to ensure that it does not become leavened.

If you would like to receive shmurah matzah for your seder, please do not hesitate to reach out to us. We are happy to assist you in obtaining this special matzah to enhance your Passover experience.

Warmly,
Rabbi M. Mendel Naparstek

The Full Story

Rabbi Mendel Naparstek was born and raised in Paris, France.
